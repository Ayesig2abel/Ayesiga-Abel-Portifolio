Personal Project

Maceo Acc System

Back to projects

Overview

Maceo Acc System is a shop-style accounting system that I designed on my own. It is built to help a small business keep track of its day-to-day trading in one organised place instead of scattered notebooks and spreadsheets.

The problem it solves

Small shops often record sales, stock and expenses by hand, which makes it hard to see what has been sold, what is left and whether the business is making money. This project brings those records together so the owner can check the state of the business quickly and with fewer mistakes.

Key features

  • Sales recording Capture each sale so daily trading is recorded in one place.
  • Stock tracking Keep a record of items in the shop and how they move.
  • Income and expense records Log money coming in and going out to understand the shop's finances.
  • Summaries and reports View the business position without adding up records by hand.

My role

  • Planned and designed the system on my own
  • Decided how records and screens should be organised
  • Built it as a personal project

What it shows

  • System design and problem solving
  • Database and records keeping
  • Building tools around real business needs
