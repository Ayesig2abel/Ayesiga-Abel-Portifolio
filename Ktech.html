Personal Project

Ktech Support System

Back to projects

Overview

Ktech Support System is an IT support system built around support tickets. Users report problems as tickets, and the support team can see, track and resolve them from one place.

The problem it solves

When IT requests come in through calls, chats and messages, issues are easily forgotten and it is hard to tell what is open, who is handling it and what has been fixed. A ticketing system gives every issue a record, so nothing is lost and support work stays organised. It comes from my day-to-day experience in IT support.

Key features

  • Ticket logging Users raise an issue and it is recorded as a support ticket.
  • Status tracking Follow each ticket from open through to resolved.
  • Assignment Tickets can be handled by the support person responsible.
  • Support history Keep a record of past issues and how they were resolved.

My role

  • Planned and designed the system on my own
  • Shaped the ticket workflow around real IT support practice
  • Built it as a personal project

What it shows

  • Hands-on understanding of IT support operations
  • Incident handling from report to resolution
  • Building tools that support my own field
